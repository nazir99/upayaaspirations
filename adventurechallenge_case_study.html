Case Study: Adventure Challenge

DTC Brand System Stabilization & Capital Raise Preparation

Client Profile

Fast-growing direct-to-consumer brand selling interactive adventure books and kits through multiple channels with 3PL fulfillment partners.

Interactive Adventure Books & Kits
Shopify Amazon Wholesale
3PL Partners: ShipBob → Givingtons

My Role

NetSuite Solution Architect / Director of Financial Systems – brought in as the internal expert to stabilize NetSuite, restore stakeholder trust, and build controls & reporting needed for capital-raise readiness.

Key Challenges

  • Original implementor gaps left the system unstable; leadership needed in-house NetSuite expertise
  • Disconnected Shopify → NetSuite → 3PL data flows causing inventory discrepancies and order delays
  • Accounting team relied on spreadsheets; approval workflows and roles were undefined
  • System mistrust hindered management reporting required for investors

Actions Taken

  1. Source-of-Truth Architecture: Established NetSuite as the authoritative ledger; redesigned data flows so Shopify/Amazon orders route through NetSuite before the 3PL, ensuring consistent SKU, lot, and quantity data.
  2. Integration Stabilization: Finalized ShipBob integration and later migrated to Givingtons, maintaining 99%+ data sync success and reducing inventory variances.
  3. Controls & Workflows: Built role-based approval workflows for purchasing, vendor bills, and journal entries; authored SOPs and swim-lane diagrams for Accounting and Ops.
  4. Management Reporting: Created SuiteAnalytics dashboards for sales, margin, and inventory turns—used in investor presentations to secure growth capital.
  5. Team Enablement: Conducted training for Accounting, Ops, and Customer Service; cut external support tickets by 70% within two months.
