Case Study: Altimetrik

IPO Readiness & Financial Systems Transformation

Client Profile

Global digital-engineering firm preparing for an IPO with operations spanning North America, Europe, and Asia.

~$700M Revenue
Multi-continental operations across 3 regions

My Role

Financial Systems Manager / Solution Architect – guided NetSuite toward IPO-readiness, stabilized post-go-live performance, and redesigned Procure-to-Pay (P2P) for a distributed finance and procurement organization.

Key Challenges

  • IPO timetable required SEC-grade controls and audit-ready evidence within 9 months
  • P2P approvals inconsistent across regions; manual workarounds delaying vendor payments
  • Recent go-live still unstable—script errors, integration glitches, and low user confidence
  • Close process exceeded 15 days; auditors and leadership required faster, reliable reporting

Actions Taken

  1. IPO-Readiness Roadmap: Conducted gap assessment with KPMG & Deloitte; mapped NetSuite configuration to SOX/ITGC requirements and built phased remediation plan.
  2. ITGC Audit: Conducted ITGC with Grant Thornton
  3. Global P2P Redesign: Implemented tiered approval workflows (amount, region, BU) and automated three-way-match; delivered region-specific training sessions.
  4. Go-Live Stabilization Sprint: Led 6-week hyper-care program, resolving >200 open tickets and boosting integration success to 99.7%.
  5. Close-Process Acceleration: Introduced automated accruals, JE templates, and KPI dashboards; empowered finance to self-serve variance analysis.
