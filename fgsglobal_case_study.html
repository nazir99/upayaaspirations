FGS Global

NetSuite OneWorld Transformation & Process Optimization

Executive Summary

Delivered comprehensive NetSuite transformation for global strategic communications firm, reducing month-end close from 9 to 5 days while establishing scalable processes for international expansion and KKR acquisition integration.

Client Profile

Industry: Strategic Communications

Structure: Global firm with 10+ subsidiaries

Ownership: Recently acquired by KKR

Requirements: Public-company rigor with multi-subsidiary NetSuite OneWorld

Engagement Role

Interim NetSuite Director & Solution Architect — Appointed to bridge Finance, Operations, and external vendors while serving as a supporting member of the project Steering Committee to rescue and scale the ERP environment.

Key Business Challenges

  • Poor initial solution design with fragmented Order-to-Cash (OTC) and billing workflows across teams
  • Month-end close process stuck at 9 days, heavily reliant on manual journal entries and spreadsheet reconciliations
  • Absence of unified change-management process resulting in limited documentation and low user confidence
  • Global subsidiaries onboarding without standardized, repeatable implementation model
  • Multiple satellite systems (HRIS, travel-management, FP&A) requiring real-time integration to NetSuite

Strategic Actions Implemented

  1. Advanced Revenue Management (ARM) & OTC Redesign: Architected unified ARM configuration that standardized revenue schedules and established template for all future subsidiary implementations. Delivered comprehensive documentation and conducted cross-functional team training.
  2. Financial Standard Operating Procedures: Authored end-to-end close procedures, approval matrices, and change-management workflows in Confluence to ensure visibility and auditability across the organization.
  3. Enterprise Architecture Governance: Established weekly Architecture Review Board participation and provided ongoing guidance for HRIS and travel-portal integrations, ensuring data consistency and optimal system performance.
  4. Executive Steering Committee Leadership: Presented solution-design alternatives, ticket-prioritization updates, and quality/timeline assessments at monthly meetings while facilitating risk escalation and executive decision-making processes.
